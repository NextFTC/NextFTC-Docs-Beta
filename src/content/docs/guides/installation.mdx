# Installation

In the Teamcode `build.gradle`, add the two lines below to your `dependencies` block

<Tabs syncKey="language">
<TabItem label=".gradle">

```groovy
implementation 'dev.nextftc.v2:robot:0.1.0-rc.1'
implementation 'com.pedropathing:ivy:1.0.0'
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label=".gradle.kts">

```kotlin
implementation("dev.nextftc.v2:robot:0.1.0-rc.1")
implementation("com.pedropathing:ivy:1.0.0")
```

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Then, press the `Sync Now` popup that appears at the top of your screen, wait a few seconds for wait to finish, and... your good to go!
